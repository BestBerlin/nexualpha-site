## Official-source facts checked

- TreasuryDirect says marketable Treasury securities can be bought through TreasuryDirect or through a bank, broker, or dealer, and identifies Treasury marketable securities by CUSIP. Source: https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/buying-a-marketable-security/
- TreasuryDirect says TreasuryDirect is the official U.S. government application for buying and holding Notes, Bonds, Bills, TIPS and FRNs, and that TreasuryDirect marketable purchases require a minimum bid of $100. Source: https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/buying-a-marketable-security/
- SEC staff statement says tokenized securities can be issuer-sponsored or third-party sponsored, and the structure/rights differ by model. Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826-statement-tokenized-securities
- SEC staff statement says a third-party tokenized security may represent indirect ownership via a securities entitlement or synthetic exposure and may introduce third-party risk. Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826-statement-tokenized-securities
- BlackRock lists official BUIDL token addresses and warns users to verify every character of token addresses. Source: https://www.blackrock.com/corporate/compliance/scams-and-fraud/blackrock-token-addresses
- Franklin Templeton's FOBXX page says Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund invests at least 99.5% of total assets in U.S. government securities, cash and repurchase agreements collateralized by government securities or cash, and identifies fund number BENJI. Source: https://www.franklintempleton.com/investments/options/money-market-funds/products/29386/SINGLCLASS/franklin-on-chain-u-s-government-money-fund/FOBXX
- Franklin Templeton's summary prospectus says the transfer agent maintains the official record of share ownership through a blockchain-integrated recordkeeping system, and that Fund shares recorded in that system remain under transfer-agent control with ability to correct unauthorized transactions or limit transferability. Source: https://www.franklintempleton.com/forms-literature/download-preview/9001-PSUM
- Franklin Templeton's summary prospectus says the Fund seeks to preserve $1.00 per share but cannot guarantee it, and an investment is not FDIC-insured or otherwise government-guaranteed. Source: https://www.franklintempleton.com/forms-literature/download-preview/9001-PSUM
