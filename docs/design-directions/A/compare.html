Our verdict

Choose Binance if liquidity and variety matter most. Tighter spreads on large pairs and the longest list of tokens make it the safer default for most traders.

Choose OKX if you pay taker fees often or want a multichain wallet in the same account. The saving is small per trade but compounds at volume.
