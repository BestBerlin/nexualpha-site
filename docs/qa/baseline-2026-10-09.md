### Real bugs found by the contract tests
- **B1. The Worker SEO projection is stale** (`priority-venue-seo`, `worker-compare-faq-schema`).
  - `src/data/compare-faq-locales.js` localized the ar and ru compare FAQ (السبوت/العقود الآجلة, спот/фьючерсы), but the generated block in `worker/index.js` still serves the old loanword copy in FAQ JSON-LD. Visible FAQ and schema now differ on ar and ru compare pages.
  - Fix: `node scripts/sync-priority-venue-seo.mjs` (changes 4 lines; it is an SEO field, so it needs the SEO owner or user OK).
  - Root cause: `build.yml` runs the vite builds directly and skips the generators that `npm run build` runs (`sync-priority-venue-seo`, campaign/listing fetch, funding matrix, news summary, llms). Nothing catches the drift.
  - Suggested gate: add `node scripts/sync-priority-venue-seo.mjs --check` to `build.yml`.
- Observation, not proven a bug: `r46-collection-hub-cta`. The `/factbook` hero lost its primary research CTA (`factbook-collection-hero-primary`) in the v6 hierarchy; only the secondary one is left. This is a CRO decision to confirm.
