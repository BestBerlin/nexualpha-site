## 0. Executive summary

1. **Chainalysis 2026 index (published 2026-09-23).** The top 20 is Brazil, US, Nigeria, Japan, Korea, India, Ukraine, Thailand, South Africa, Canada, Mexico, China, Germany, Indonesia, Australia, Russia, UK, Vietnam, Philippines, Türkiye. Pakistan dropped out of the public top 20 after placing #3 in 2025. The 2026 methodology was rebuilt (117 countries, 4 equally weighted sub-indices), so year-on-year rank changes are not like-for-like. Sources: https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/ (2026-09-23) and https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2025-global-crypto-adoption-index/ (2025-09-02).
2. **Best new-locale bet: Ukrainian (`uk`).** Adoption is #7, almost all partners serve Ukraine outside occupied regions, Telegram dominates, and Ukrainian-language exchange queries are almost uncompleted in Google, which means little competition (see §5).
3. **Urdu (`ur`) is worth doing, with a regulatory caveat.** Pakistan's PVARA licensing deadline passed on 2026-09-05. Only Binance and HTX are confirmed to hold NOCs, and Gate's terms list Pakistan as restricted. Urdu demand is real but religiously framed: autocomplete shows "شرعی حکم" (Sharia ruling) and "حلال یا حرام" (halal or haram).
4. **Brazil has a hard date: the Banco Central do Brasil (BCB) authorization filing deadline is 2026-10-30.** Offshore exchanges must file, migrate users to an authorized entity, or exit. Deepen `pt-BR` now, but build the partner pages so a partner can be switched off per country within hours.
5. **Africa verdicts.**
   - Go-careful: Nigeria (English), South Africa (English), Kenya (English plus a Swahili pilot), Ghana (English).
   - Careful/hold: Tanzania, Uganda.
   - Avoid: Ethiopia (National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) ban, July 2026), Egypt (Law 194/2020 criminalises promoting crypto), Morocco (2017 prohibition still operative until Bill 42.25 passes).
   - Two promotion laws matter for us as a publisher: Nigeria's SEC requires prior approval and disclosure for crypto ads and influencer promotion, and Egypt bans promotion outright.
6. **Avoid these candidate markets:** Malaysia (MEXC, Bitget, OKX, KuCoin, and Gate all exclude it), Kazakhstan/Uzbekistan (multiple partners exclude them and Kazakhstan blocks unlicensed exchanges), Nepal (ban), Algeria (Law 25-10 ban), Iran (sanctions). Venezuela is careful: OFAC sanctions, and MEXC (Spanish terms), Gate, and HTX exclude it.
7. **Messaging.** Telegram stays the core automated channel. WhatsApp Channels has no official posting API (third-party gateways use unofficial device-linking), and Meta's business policies restrict crypto, so treat WhatsApp as manual, informational broadcast only. Vietnam blocked Telegram in June 2025, which puts our `vi` channel at risk; Zalo is the local alternative, but its policy is unverified.
8. **MEXC KYC.** MEXC's own User Agreement requires identity documents. MEXC's own learn page (2026-10-09) says the Help Center "publishes no withdrawal allowance for unverified accounts". Other MEXC pages cite 10 BTC/day or 1,000 USDT, so **do not publish a no-KYC limit as fact**. Hyperliquid has no KYC; it is a non-custodial interface whose terms bar the US, Ontario, sanctioned territories, and citizens of those territories. Backpack has mandatory KYC.

### 1.1 Nigeria: go (careful). English, plus Hausa/Yoruba/Pidgin later
- **Adoption (verified):** Chainalysis 2026 #3 overall, #1 in P2P economy, #1 in cross-border flows. https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/ (2026-09-23)
- **Regulation (secondary):**
  - The Investments and Securities Act 2025 classifies digital assets as securities and requires SEC registration for digital asset exchanges and operators. https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/securities/1607978/a-review-of-the-investment-and-securities-act-2025
  - Approval-in-principle was granted to local exchanges Busha and Quidax. https://tynmagazine.com/?p=203403
  - The SEC declared Binance's Nigerian operations illegal in June/July 2023. https://www.theblock.co/post/234055/nigeria-regulator-says-binance-not-registered-and-must-stop-soliciting-investors-reuters
- **Promotion rules: the most relevant for us (secondary).**
  - Under the SEC's revised Digital Asset Rules (scheduled effective 2025-06-30), influencers need prior approval for crypto ads, must check that the platform is SEC-licensed, must disclose payment, and must label the content as sponsored.
  - The reported minimum penalty is ₦10m or up to 3 years in prison.
  - Sources: https://cointelegraph.com/news/nigeria-sec-marketing-promotions-crypto-vasp and https://nairametrics.com/?p=513623.
  - **Implication:** referral CTAs that target Nigerian users and point to unregistered platforms are a real legal exposure.
- **Partners and rails:**
  - Binance discontinued all NGN services in March 2024 and disabled P2P in February 2024. https://gazettengr.com/binance-disables-p2p-function-amid-clampdown-from-nigerian-authorities/
  - OKX exited in August 2024. It reportedly restored full access, including P2P, in July 2026, but its regulatory basis is unclear (**unverified**, no official OKX notice found). https://innovation-village.com/okx-restores-p2p-trading-and-full-access-in-nigeria/
  - Autocomplete shows "okx back to nigeria", "bybit nigeria p2p" and "bitget p2p nigeria" (§5).
- **Content languages:** English first. Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo and Pidgin are possible later; their demand is unverified.
- **Verdict: GO-CAREFUL.**
  - Serve with the existing `en` locale and a Nigeria hub covering fees, P2P safety, ISA 2025 and SEC status per partner.
  - Disclosure must be visible.
  - Do not run paid influencer ads in Nigeria without legal review.

  - As of December 2025: 512 applications, 300 approved, and 81 investigations into unlicensed activity. https://iol.co.za/business-report/companies/2025-12-16-fsca-approves-300-crypto-service-providers-intensifies-action-against-unlicensed-operators/ (2025-12-16) and https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/publications/2026/01/fsca-update-on-licensing-and-supervision-of-crypto-asset-service-providers (2026-01-19)
  - The FSCA warned about Binance (2021) and Bybit (2022) as not authorised for intermediary services. https://www.news24.com/business/markets/sa-public-warned-about-doing-business-with-worlds-largest-cryptocurrency-exchange-20210903
- **Partners:**
  - Bybit Pay expanded to South Africa via MoneyBadger in April 2026. https://www.bolsamania.com/nota-de-prensa/mercados/bybit-pay-expands-to-south-africa-with-moneybadger-enabling-nationwide-crypto-qr-payments--22367973.html
  - FSCA licence status of OKX, Bybit, Binance and Bitget is **unverified**. Check the FSCA CASP register before each claim.
- **Rails:** EFT, PayShap and P2P. VALR and Luno are local licensed exchanges and not our partners (secondary/affiliate source).
- **Verdict: GO-CAREFUL** via `en`. State FSCA licence status per partner, or write "not verified".

### 1.6 Uganda: careful. English, Luganda/Swahili

### 1.7 Ethiopia: avoid. Amharic
