# Beginner path evidence boundaries

- Partner priority is the owner's instruction: Bitget, OKX, Binance; Türkiye: OKX, Binance, Bybit. It is an editorial ordering, not a safety rating.
- Country exclusions and commercial eligibility reuse `partner-geo-policy.js` and the source-bound CTA projection. Licence/entity/status rows reuse `licenses.js`; operating-list membership is not licence approval.
- Invite codes, benefits and backup destinations come from the existing registry/projections. Unverified fee benefits are not promoted as guaranteed savings. Neutral locales render no codes or `/go` anchors.
- Official OKX post-registration rule: [Referral Program FAQ](https://www.okx.com/en-gb/help/okx-referral-program-faq), read 2026-10-14. Sections 13 and 17 state that the relationship normally cannot be added/changed after signup and the code must be applied before completion. This is an OKX rule, not an all-exchange rule.
- Taxes: answers direct readers to their own tax authority and recordkeeping. When the selected country is US, the FAQ points to the official [IRS digital-assets resource](https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets), read 2026-10-14. No universal tax treatment is asserted.
- No sourced account-specific deposit option or processing time is available for every country/venue combination. The steps therefore describe checking the account's current card/bank/P2P options and shown processing status rather than inventing rails, limits or times.
- Tooltip definitions are compact projections from the existing glossary, with the source content locale retained when a native definition is unavailable.
- The checker performs a registry-domain comparison, not a network/security audit. A matching domain does not verify the page, support message, account eligibility or fund safety.
