# Level 4 visual identity

## Style Prompt

A restrained financial editorial explainer: asymmetric typography, generous negative space, large section numerals and custom drawn interface demonstrations. Each shot has one teaching point. Landscape pairs a left headline with a right demonstration; portrait stacks the headline, demonstration and caption above the platform controls. No stock imagery or exchange interface imitation.

## Colors

Canvas #0b0d10; foreground #eef2f9; secondary #b6c0d0; blue accent #4f7cff; positive #3ddc97; negative #ff5c5c; caution #f8bb6a. Dark surfaces #151b25 and hairlines #344052.

## Typography

PingFang SC for Chinese, Inter for Latin (system sans fallback). Headlines 94px landscape, 92px portrait; body 36–44px; captions 42px. Numbers use tabular spacing.

## Motion

Three simultaneous layers maximum: ambient grid, content entrance, teaching interaction. 0.4–0.7 second entrances with power3, expo and restrained back easing. A 0.4-second blue shutter covers beat-aligned scene cuts; no full-page fades. Finite GSAP timelines, no wall clock, random, or CSS animations. Intro lasts exactly 3 seconds. Estimated durations round up to 30fps frames; transitions and illustration events adapt to replacement scene timing.

## Safe areas

Landscape 112px horizontal / 64px top / 80px bottom. Portrait 80px sides / 100px top; reserve bottom 320px for platform UI. Captions sit above this reserved area, separated from illustrations.

## What NOT to Do

No gradients on text, neon glow, decorative dashboard clutter, generic equal-card grids, plain slide fades, fake real screenshots, real identity data, voice/music generation or service login. The phone must say “示意画面，非真实界面”. CTA disclosure must remain visible alongside the QR code.
